# AIRUUN Microsite
## Thailand Wellbeing Platform Research & Current Practices

**Research date:** 22 July 2026  
**Purpose:** Market benchmarking and product-strategy input for the AIRUUN client presentation and discovery workshop.

---

# 1. Executive Summary

Thailand's insurance and healthcare market is moving beyond policy servicing toward broader ecosystems that combine health services, everyday wellbeing, digital engagement, rewards, personalization, and partner services.

No single platform reviewed fully matches the AIRUUN vision. Existing platforms generally emphasize one or more of the following:

- Health activity tracking
- Points, status, and rewards
- Premium-related benefits
- Telemedicine and digital healthcare
- Personalized health planning
- Policy and customer services
- LINE-based access
- Lifestyle content and daily activities

This creates a distinct opportunity for AIRUUN.

Rather than competing as another insurance application, health-score platform, or telemedicine gateway, AIRUUN can occupy a different position:

> **A content-led wellbeing relationship platform built around meaning, belonging, reflection, and long-term engagement.**

The strongest strategic opportunity is to combine:

1. Thai-rooted wellbeing philosophy
2. The five AIRUUN wellbeing pillars
3. The Dawn Thread as a recurring ritual
4. Low-friction entry through LINE and personalized links
5. Gradual, transparent personalization
6. Curated activities and member stories
7. Clear consent and responsible data use
8. A phased path from content platform to intelligent wellbeing ecosystem

# 2. Thailand Market Direction

## 2.1 The Market Is Moving Beyond Insurance

The conventional insurance application focuses on transactional services such as:

- Viewing policy information
- Paying premiums
- Submitting claims
- Downloading documents
- Finding hospitals
- Contacting an agent or service center

Current platforms increasingly extend beyond these functions into:

```text
Insurance Services
        ↓
Digital Healthcare
        ↓
Lifestyle and Prevention
        ↓
Daily Wellbeing
        ↓
Rewards and Recognition
        ↓
Long-term Engagement
```

The direction is clear: insurers and healthcare providers are trying to become more relevant between major policy, claim, or hospital moments.

## 2.2 Common Strategic Objectives

The reviewed platforms use wellbeing features to support several business objectives:

- Increase customer interaction frequency
- Encourage healthier behavior
- Strengthen brand relevance
- Create reasons to return to the app
- Connect customers with ecosystem partners
- Generate behavioral insight
- Increase retention and loyalty
- Position the brand as a health or wellbeing partner

# 3. Thailand Platform Benchmark

## 3.1 AIA Vitality

### Core Model

AIA Vitality uses a behavioral wellness and rewards model. Members earn points from healthy choices and activities, improve their membership status, and unlock rewards and insurance-related benefits.

### Current Practices

- Health activity and behavior tracking
- Points and membership status
- Monthly rewards
- Partner privileges and discounts
- Health assessments
- Active challenges
- Integration with the AIA+ application
- Benefits aligned with member status for eligible insurance products

### Strength

The platform creates a clear relationship between:

```text
Healthy Action → Points → Status → Reward → Repeat Action
```

### Learning for AIRUUN

AIRUUN does not need to copy a financial reward model, but it should make progress visible.

Possible AIRUUN progress signals:

- Completed today's Dawn Thread
- Practiced three days this week
- Explored a new wellbeing pillar
- Saved a meaningful story
- Joined a live activity
- Completed a monthly wellbeing journey

---

## 3.2 Muang Thai Fit Rewards

### Core Model

Muang Thai Life's Fit Rewards converts health-related actions into Fit Points. Eligible activities include step tracking, exercise-minute logging, health-check result submission, and other program activities.

Fit Points may be used for renewal premium discounts under program conditions.

### Current Practices

- Step tracking
- Exercise-minute tracking
- Health-check data submission
- Campaign-based challenges
- Fit Points
- Premium-related incentives
- E-vouchers and promotional rewards
- Integration within the MTL Click ecosystem

### Strength

The program turns abstract health goals into measurable actions with a tangible outcome.

### Learning for AIRUUN

Every AIRUUN pillar should include small actions that users can understand and complete.

Examples:

- Two-minute breathing practice
- Short reflection
- Family conversation prompt
- Simple financial wellbeing check-in
- Community kindness action
- Event registration
- Story contribution

---

## 3.3 Well by Samitivej

### Core Model

Well by Samitivej is positioned as a healthy-lifestyle application that uses health data, AI-supported analysis, and expert knowledge to provide personalized food and fitness plans.

### Current Practices

- Personalized health plans
- AI-supported health-data analysis
- Expert input
- Food recommendations
- Exercise recommendations
- Smartwatch integration
- Apple Health and Google Fit connectivity
- Medical-record access for Samitivej users
- Daily healthy missions
- Rewards for completing healthy activities

### Strength

The platform combines technology with trusted human expertise instead of presenting AI as a standalone solution.

### Learning for AIRUUN

AIRUUN should use AI to improve relevance, not to replace professional judgment.

Recommended principle:

> **AI assists discovery and recommendation; trusted people remain responsible for sensitive health, financial, and community content.**

---

## 3.4 OCEAN CONNECT and OCEAN LIFE Digital Healthcare

### Core Model

OCEAN LIFE uses LINE OCEAN CONNECT as an access layer for customer and digital-healthcare services.

### Current Practices

- Access through LINE
- Customer-service connection
- Policy-related services
- Digital healthcare
- Telemedicine
- Telepharmacy
- Healthcare-partner ecosystem
- Medication delivery for eligible services
- Multiple healthcare providers connected through one brand ecosystem

### Strength

The platform meets Thai users in an application they already use instead of relying only on a standalone app.

### Learning for AIRUUN

LINE should be treated as AIRUUN's engagement and re-entry layer, while the microsite provides the deeper branded experience.

Recommended structure:

```text
LINE Personalized Message
            ↓
AIRUUN Deep Link
            ↓
One-Tap Wellbeing Choice
            ↓
Personalized Microsite Experience
            ↓
LINE Reminder and Return Loop
```

---

## 3.5 LINE HEALTH

### Core Model

LINE HEALTH was officially launched in Thailand in September 2025 through partnerships with multiple telemedicine providers. It gives users access to doctors and healthcare services through a LINE Official Account.

### Current Practices

- Embedded service inside a familiar platform
- Multiple service providers
- Central access point
- Healthcare knowledge
- Telemedicine journeys
- Reduced application-installation friction
- Partner-led service ecosystem

### Strength

The model demonstrates the value of using an existing high-frequency platform as an access layer.

### Learning for AIRUUN

AIRUUN should avoid forcing full registration before users experience value.

Recommended sequence:

1. Receive invitation
2. See a meaningful opening question
3. Choose an interest
4. Receive an immediate personalized result
5. Request login only when necessary
6. Bring users back through LINE

---

## 3.6 UP by Thai Life Insurance

### Core Model

UP is presented as a lifestyle platform supporting physical and mental wellbeing while encouraging a positive impact on society.

### Current Practices Identified

- Lifestyle positioning beyond insurance transactions
- Physical wellbeing
- Mental wellbeing
- Positive social impact
- Daily or ongoing lifestyle engagement
- Brand relationship outside conventional policy servicing

### Strength

The platform illustrates how an insurance brand can expand into a broader lifestyle territory.

### Learning for AIRUUN

AIRUUN's five pillars can create a broader definition of wellbeing than fitness alone, particularly through relationships, financial balance, generosity, and belonging.

## 3.7 Thai Life Insurance App

### Core Model

The main Thai Life Insurance application combines policy transactions, claims, privileges, healthcare access, and newer wellbeing-related services.

### Current Practices

- Policy overview
- Digital claims
- Electronic documents
- Hospital search
- Telepharmacy
- Telemedicine-related services
- Family policy management
- Preliminary health assessment features
- Lifestyle privileges

### Strength

It provides a broad service ecosystem around the customer relationship.

### Learning for AIRUUN

AIRUUN should be clear about its role relative to existing TGH service platforms.

It may serve primarily as:

- Brand experience
- Wellbeing content platform
- Member engagement platform
- Community and activity platform

It should not duplicate policy-management functions unless integration is strategically required.

# 5. Current Product Practices

## 5.1 Reduce Entry Friction

Common approaches:

- LINE entry
- Deep links
- OTP verification
- One-time account linking
- Simple onboarding
- Value before registration
- Progressive profile collection

### AIRUUN Application

Avoid opening with a long form.

Recommended first interaction:

> **What would you like more of in this chapter of life?**

The user chooses one need and immediately receives a relevant experience.

---

## 5.2 Build a Repeatable Ritual

Content alone is not sufficient to create retention.

Platforms create return behavior through:

- Daily missions
- Weekly challenges
- Health reminders
- Progress tracking
- Personalized suggestions
- Reward cycles
- New content drops

### AIRUUN Application

The Dawn Thread should become a repeatable wellbeing ritual rather than only an article section.

Suggested daily format:

1. A short reflection
2. One small practice
3. One meaningful story or insight
4. A personal check-in
5. A suggested next action
6. A gentle return invitation

---

## 5.3 Connect Content to Action

A modern wellbeing platform does not end with reading.

Every content item should lead naturally to a next step:

- Try a practice
- Save for later
- Reflect
- Share privately
- Join an activity
- Register for a session
- Explore another pillar
- Submit a story

### AIRUUN Application

Each content template should include a contextual CTA rather than one generic CTA across the platform.

---

## 5.4 Make Progress Visible

Users are more likely to return when they can see continuity.

Progress does not have to mean competitive points.

Possible recognition models:

- Journey completion
- Weekly consistency
- Pillar exploration
- Practice streak
- Reflection history
- Activity participation
- Personal milestones

### AIRUUN Application

Use gentle recognition instead of aggressive gamification.

Recommended tone:

- Encouraging
- Non-competitive
- Private by default
- Meaningful rather than transactional
- Free from health shaming

---

## 5.5 Personalize Gradually

Leading systems generally develop personalization in stages.

### Level 1 — Declared Preference

Use information users intentionally provide:

- Selected wellbeing need
- Preferred pillar
- Preferred content format
- Available time
- Life-stage interest

### Level 2 — Behavioral Personalization

Use platform activity:

- Content viewed
- Completion
- Save
- Share
- Event booking
- Return frequency
- Pillar exploration

### Level 3 — Contextual Recommendation

Combine preferences and behavior:

- Current interest
- Time of day
- Recent activity
- Content duration
- Preferred format
- Journey stage

### Level 4 — AI-Assisted Personalization

Use AI to:

- Rank relevant content
- Identify changing interests
- Recommend return journeys
- Summarize anonymized community signals
- Assist content tagging
- Support editorial planning

### AIRUUN Principle

> **Collect meaningful and consented signals before investing in complex AI.**

---

## 5.6 Use Ecosystem Partners

Most platforms do not build all health services internally.

Common partner categories:

- Hospitals
- Telemedicine providers
- Pharmacies
- Fitness brands
- Nutrition specialists
- Mental wellbeing experts
- Activity organizers
- Lifestyle and reward partners

### AIRUUN Application

AIRUUN should first establish its identity and member relationship, then connect relevant partners when they strengthen the five-pillar experience.

---

## 5.7 Separate Wellbeing from Hard Selling

A major risk for insurer-led wellbeing platforms is that users may perceive the experience as disguised product promotion.

### Recommended Practice

- Deliver value before offers
- Clearly label commercial content
- Keep financial wellbeing educational and non-sales-led
- Avoid using sensitive behavior as a direct sales trigger without appropriate consent
- Use contextual service recommendations only when relevant
- Separate editorial governance from campaign objectives

---

## 5.8 Treat Consent as Part of the Experience

Consent should not be only a legal screen.

Users should understand:

- What information is collected
- Why it is collected
- How it changes recommendations
- Whether information is shared
- How to update preferences
- How to withdraw consent
- What happens to historical data

### AIRUUN Application

Use progressive consent:

1. Essential platform consent
2. Personalization consent
3. Marketing consent
4. Community contribution consent
5. Sensitive-data consent when applicable

---

## 5.9 Combine Technology with Human Trust

AI and automation can improve efficiency, but sensitive wellbeing experiences require human oversight.

Human roles may include:

- Content editor
- Medical reviewer
- Financial-content reviewer
- Community moderator
- Data-protection owner
- Activity operator
- Customer-support team

### AIRUUN Application

Human governance should be designed together with platform functionality.

# 11. Risks and Watchouts

## Product Risks

- Trying to launch all ecosystem functions at once
- Building a content library without a return mechanism
- Overcomplicated onboarding
- Unclear relationship with existing TGH applications
- AI introduced before sufficient data exists
- Features without operational owners

## Trust Risks

- Wellbeing content perceived as sales promotion
- Unclear consent
- Sensitive-data use without adequate explanation
- Over-personalization
- Medical or financial advice without professional governance
- Community content without moderation

## Operational Risks

- Insufficient launch content
- No publishing workflow
- Unclear moderation SLA
- Activity booking without attendance operations
- Fragmented analytics
- Lack of CRM/API readiness

- Are APIs ready?

- What security standards apply?

# 14. Conclusion

The Thai market confirms that consumers are increasingly offered health and wellbeing experiences beyond traditional insurance services.

However, the dominant models remain centered on transactions, physical-health behavior, healthcare access, points, or rewards.

AIRUUN can differentiate by establishing a deeper emotional and relational territory:

> **A trusted digital place where members return for small moments of wellbeing, meaningful content, and a sense of belonging.**

The recommended strategy is therefore not to reproduce an existing wellness application. It is to selectively adopt proven practices—low-friction access, daily rituals, visible progress, progressive personalization, trusted partners, and responsible data use—while building a distinctly AIRUUN experience around its five pillars and Thai-rooted wellbeing philosophy.
