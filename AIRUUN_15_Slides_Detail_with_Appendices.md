# Slide 1 — AIRUUN Is Moving Beyond a Microsite

## Proposed Headline

**From a Microsite to a Living Wisdom + Wellbeing Platform**

## Core Message

AIRUUN should not be developed as a conventional content website or short-term campaign microsite.

Its current vision includes:

- Daily content and Daily Dawn
- Five wellbeing pillars
- Member identity
- Personalization
- Saved content and history
- Events and physical-space programmes
- Community participation
- LINE and CRM communication
- Analytics and member insight
- Future AI recommendation

These capabilities require product logic, data structures, identity, CMS, integrations, analytics and continuous operation.

## Why This Matters

A microsite is usually designed to:

- Communicate a campaign
- Publish static information
- Generate traffic
- Drive a short-term CTA
- Operate for a limited period

AIRUUN is expected to:

- Recognize different user states
- Learn member needs
- Recommend relevant experiences
- Support return usage
- Connect digital and physical participation
- Build long-term relationship value

## Research-backed Rationale

Digital wellbeing initiatives require more than software. They depend on coordinated technology, people, governance, content, operations and measurement.

## TWF Sales Message

> **TWF is not proposing another website. We are proposing the product and technology foundation that allows AIRUUN to operate, learn and grow over time.**

# Appendix A1 — Why AIRUUN Is More Than a Microsite

## Supports Slide 1

| Dimension | Campaign Microsite | AIRUUN Platform |
|---|---|---|
| Primary purpose | Communicate a campaign | Build an ongoing relationship |
| User state | Mostly anonymous | Guest, member, policyholder, admin |
| Content | Static or campaign-based | Scheduled and continuous |
| Identity | Usually unnecessary | Required for continuity and personalization |
| Data | Traffic and conversion | Intent, preference, behaviour and participation |
| Experience | Read and click | Read, practise, save, book, return and contribute |
| Operation | Limited period | Continuous editorial and programme operation |
| Integration | Minimal | LINE, CRM, consent, analytics and events |
| Lifespan | Short to medium term | Long-term product roadmap |
| Success | Visit or conversion | Usefulness, return, action and relationship |

## Risk of Treating AIRUUN as a Microsite

- Scope expansion after development starts
- CMS unable to support future content types
- Login added too late
- Weak data foundation
- Integration underestimation
- Event flow disconnected from product
- Community added without governance
- Architecture rebuild in later phases

## Recommended Client Answer

AIRUUN can start small, but it must start with the right foundation.

# Appendix B4 — Market Gaps and AIRUUN White Space

## Gap 1 — Wellness Is Often Reduced to Physical Health

AIRUUN opportunity:

- Whole-life wellbeing across heart, body, family, money and contribution

## Gap 2 — Content and Services Are Fragmented

AIRUUN opportunity:

- One identity
- One taxonomy
- One member journey
- One analytics model
- One digital-to-physical relationship

## Gap 3 — Community Is Missing or Operationally Heavy

AIRUUN opportunity:

- Start with curated participation
- Scale only when governance is ready

## Gap 4 — Insurer Wellness Often Links to Rewards or Products

AIRUUN opportunity:

- Separate core wellbeing from selling
- Make TGH transparent as sponsor

## Gap 5 — Thai Wisdom Is Usually Content, Not Product Logic

AIRUUN opportunity:

- Daily Dawn
- Seasonal rhythm
- Practices
- Heritage journeys
- Physical workshops
- Intergenerational stories

## White Space

> **A non-sales, insurer-sponsored relationship platform combining Thai wisdom, whole-life wellbeing, guided action, physical programmes and progressive community participation.**

# Final Strategic Conclusion

Thailand already has strong examples of:

- Wellness rewards
- Policy-service applications
- Digital healthcare
- Public-health ecosystems
- Mental-wellness support
- Active-ageing communities
- Structured behaviour-change programmes

AIRUUN should not compete directly with only one of these.

It should combine:

- The identity continuity of an insurer platform
- The credibility of public-health content
- The human connection of a safe support community
- The online-to-offline participation of an active-ageing platform
- The structure of guided programmes
- The cultural grounding of Thai wisdom
- The breadth of whole-life wellbeing
- The trust of a non-sales experience

> **AIRUUN should not be the platform that publishes the most content. It should be the platform that helps members take the most meaningful next action.**
