3 Phases

One product foundation. Each phase adds a new maturity layer without rebuilding the core platform.

How the Platform Evolves

AIRUUN should not be understood as three sets of features. Each phase creates a new capability layer on top of the previous one. The platform evolves from delivering useful content, to remembering the relationship, to intelligently helping each member discover what may be most useful next.
