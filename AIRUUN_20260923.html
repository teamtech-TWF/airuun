AIRUUN · Member Journey
04 / 15
The big-picture member journey

One need becomes a journey from discovery to belonging.

01 · Discover

Find what feels relevant now.

Enter through a familiar channel and explore what feels useful or meaningful.

AIRUUN learns: entry source and explicit intent.

02 · Participate

Take the next meaningful step.

Engage through content, practices, journeys, activities or programmes.

AIRUUN learns: pillar interest, start and completion.

03 · Connect

Be part of something shared.

Join people, experts or communities around shared interests and experiences.

AIRUUN learns: preference, return and journey progress.

04 · Contribute

Create value across connected communities.

Share experience, knowledge, time, support or participation back into the community.

AIRUUN learns: contribution, attendance and continued participation.

Value appears before identityThe journey grows only when the next step is useful

Appendix A7 · Shared vs Controlled Data
A7 / A14
Network governance

Shared experiences do not require shared identity data.

Shared across the network

Public content, journeys, programmes, open events, expert sessions, volunteer opportunities and approved stories.

Controlled by policy & consent

Identity, community membership, behaviour, attendance history, private discussion and CRM/CDP records.

Define ownership, access, consent purpose and retention before connecting systemsShare experience first
