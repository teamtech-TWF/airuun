# Vision

Today's insurance companies interact with customers only a few times each year.

```
Purchase Policy
      ↓
Receive Policy
      ↓
Claim
      ↓
Renew
```

This creates limited opportunities to build long-term relationships.

AIRUUN changes this model.

Instead of being present only when customers need insurance, TGH becomes a trusted wellbeing companion that delivers value every day through AI-powered personalization.
