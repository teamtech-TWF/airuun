03
Relationship Opportunity

Create value between insurance transactions.

Insurance relationships are usually visible only at purchase, premium, renewal, service, claim, or promotion moments. AIRUUN adds a useful relationship layer between them.

Current Relationship Transaction-led, reactive, product-centred, communication-heavy. Useful for service moments, weak for everyday relevance.
AIRUUN Relationship Need-led, preventive, voluntary, measurable, and life-centred. Useful enough to earn return visits without becoming intrusive.
Member Value Relevant knowledge, small actions, family and money reflection, safe participation.
TGH Value Brand relevance, trust, first-party insight, and permission-based continuity.
Guardrail Every interaction must provide a useful reason for the relationship to continue.
04
Audience States

One wellbeing platform, four different user states.

AIRUUN needs clear guest, member, policyholder, and operator logic from the start because trust, access, and continuity differ by state.

01 Anonymous Guest Arrives through search, social, shared content, event, or partner activity. Receives value before registration.
02 Registered Member Registers to save, continue a journey, book, connect LINE, or receive reminders.
03 Recognized Policyholder Arrives through consented channels and receives recognition without turning the experience into policy servicing.
04 Admin & Operators Manage content, events, approvals, moderation, analytics, and LINE/CRM operations.
The major differences are trust level, identity readiness, registration threshold, and communication continuity.
05
Experience Promise

Value before identity. Relationship before monetization.

AIRUUN should feel like a gift, not a sales funnel. Public value comes first; login appears only when continuity becomes useful.

Public Before Login
  • Daily Dawn sample, article, short audio, practice
  • Heritage story, programme listing, and event information
  • Clear optional next step without pressure
Login When It Adds Value
  • Save, progress, booking, reminder, personalization, contribution
  • Separate service consent from marketing consent
  • Avoid dark patterns, engagement farming, and forced registration
Reading-only users remain valid users. AIRUUN leads; TGH stays visible as sponsor.

08
Member Journey

Entry → Identity → Intent → Value → Habit.

Phase 1 does not need advanced AI. It needs a well-defined end-to-end journey and a rule-based return mechanism.

01 Entry LINE, CRM, SMS, email, search, social, event, or direct share.
02 Identity LINE Login, magic link, OTP, TGH SSO, or personalized CRM link.
03 Intent “What would you like to take care of today?” Calm, Energy, Family, Money, Giving.
04 Value Short, understandable, actionable, complete, relevant, and low-friction.
05 Habit Continue tomorrow, save, reminder, event, related story, reflection, or activity.
Phase 1 recommendation signals can rely on selected need, pillar, viewed content, saved content, event interest, and editorial priority.
09
Policyholder Journey

Recognize first. Personalize carefully.

Recognized policyholder journeys should use consented channels and light recognition, while keeping wellbeing content clearly separate from policy detail and sales triggers.

Invitation Consent-based LINE invitation Example prompt: “Would you like to have more energy in your day?”
Recognition Light member recognition LINE Login or personalized link without showing policy detail inside the experience.
First Value Kai-mapped recommendation Morning stretch, sleep reflection, short article, or relevant programme.
Continuation Digital-to-physical follow-up Save, reminder, onsite programme, feedback, related Dawn, and next action.
Keep personalization explainable. Do not convert wellbeing actions into automatic sales leads.
10
Guest Journey

Provide value first. Earn the relationship.

Guest journeys should begin with useful public content and only invite registration when the user wants continuity.

01 Discovery Search, shared article, social, public event, or partner activity.
02 Immediate Value Read or complete a short experience without login.
03 Select Need Choose a current need and receive a relevant next experience.
04 Register Only for Continuity Save, book, get a reminder, continue a journey, or join a programme.
05 Return Saved content, reminder, Daily Dawn, programme, or shared story.
Continuity, not restriction, should be the reason to register.

Phase 1 MVP

Start with a useful platform, not the entire vision.

The MVP should prove the relationship model, not attempt every future module at launch.

Core Platform
  • Homepage, AIRUUN intro, Daily Dawn, five-pillar browsing
  • Root and Heritage, room landing pages, content detail, practices
  • Programme listing, event detail, physical-space information
  • Search, filter, social sharing, and basic analytics
Member + CMS Foundation
  • Guest/member states, light login, profile, current need, preferences, saves
  • Structured content types, taxonomy, draft-review-approve-publish flow
  • Basic personalization from signals, not AI
  • Optional modules priced separately: booking, waitlist, QR, CRM sync, MINI App
The MVP is the minimum system capable of proving the AIRUUN relationship model.

Measurement

Measure the journey, not just page views.

AIRUUN should track whether members receive first value, select a need, return, attend programmes, and participate meaningfully.

Entry Source, campaign, device, session Main metric: entry-to-first-value.
Identity Login method, success, abandonment Main metric: recognition success.
Intent Selected need and pillar Main metric: intent selection.
Habit Return, reminder, continuation Main metric: 7-day and 30-day return.
Avoid forced streaks, anxiety notifications, punitive resets, and engagement farming.

Thailand Context

Thailand already has strong pieces of the wellbeing ecosystem, but not this combination.

The visible market shows strong benchmarks in wellness rewards, policy service, healthcare access, public-health content, safe support, active ageing, and guided programmes.

AIA Vitality / AIA+ Identity, tracking, points, rewards Adopt continuity and partner integration. Avoid reward-led participation as the core logic.
Insurance Service Apps Policy servicing and customer convenience Service apps manage the policy. AIRUUN should support the life around it.
YoungHappy / Sati / WHO Step-by-Step Community, safety, and guided journeys Strong reminder that belonging and structure matter as much as content volume.
ThaiHealth / Doctor Anywhere Credibility, ecosystem breadth, and expert access Useful models for sourcing, referral boundaries, and online-to-offline continuity.
No visible public benchmark currently combines Thai wisdom, whole-life wellbeing, insurer-sponsored non-sales experience, physical programmes, and progressive community participation in one platform.
23
Market White Space

AIRUUN’s white space is relationship + meaning, not another transaction layer.

The opportunity is to combine selected best practices while avoiding the typical market traps of fragmentation, rewards dependency, and shallow content-only positioning.

Platform Type Strength Typical Limitation AIRUUN Opportunity
Insurer wellness / rewards Identity, tracking, incentives Often ties wellbeing to product or reward logic Separate core wellbeing from selling
Service super apps Convenience and policy utility Journeys start from service need, not life need Support life around the policy, not duplicate servicing
Public-health / ecosystem portals Credibility and breadth Often fragmented and directory-like Create one identity, one taxonomy, one journey model
Community or guided programmes Belonging, safety, structured continuity Operationally heavy when opened too widely Start with curated participation and scale with governance
AIRUUN should not be the platform that publishes the most content. It should be the platform that helps members take the most meaningful next action.
